Fence services in Johnson City, TN

Each fence below is a different job. Pick the one that matches the yard, the animals, or the lot, then call (423) 468-2697 or send the details.

Wood fencing

Privacy, shadowbox, and board-on-board wood for yards that want a solid screen and a stain that matches the house. We measure the line, set posts for clay, and price each gate on its own.

View service

Vinyl fencing

Privacy, picket, and ranch-rail vinyl for a straight line that does not need a yearly stain. A practical fit for humid summers and freeze-thaw winters around Johnson City.

View service

Aluminum fencing

Open aluminum picket for front yards, pool barriers, and lines that have to follow a slope. It stays straight in humidity and does not ask for a paint cycle the way steel does.

View service

Chain-link fencing

Galvanized and vinyl-coated chain link for yards, dogs, and light commercial lots. A practical fence when you need containment more than a solid screen.

View service

Metal fencing

Ornamental steel for lines and gates that need more strength than aluminum. A finish you can maintain, not a rust-proof promise.

View service

Picket fencing

Open picket, in wood or vinyl, for front yards and gardens that need a boundary without a wall. Heights stay low enough to keep the street view clear.

View service

Privacy fencing

Solid wood or vinyl panels that close a backyard off from the next house. A screen for the side and rear, not a front-yard wall and not a security system.

View service

Farm fencing

Rail and woven wire for horses, cattle, and property lines on acreage. Built for a pasture, not copied from a backyard panel.

View service

Commercial fencing

Heavier chain link, ornamental rail, and vehicle gates for yards, shops, and job sites. Specified for the use of the lot, not borrowed from a residential panel.

View service

Gate installation

Walk gates and drive gates hung on posts that can carry the leaf. New openings, and repairs when a gate drags, sags, or will not latch.

View service

Fence repair

Boards, mesh, rails, and gates replaced where the rest of the line is still sound. If the posts have failed, we say so instead of covering them up.

View service
