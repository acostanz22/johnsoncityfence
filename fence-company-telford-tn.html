Fence company in Telford, TN

Telford is rural Washington County. Fence work here is usually a long pasture line, a farm gate, and sometimes a shorter screen around the house.

What fence work looks like here

We spec rail for horses and woven wire for cattle, with braces at the corners and a gate a truck can use. A backyard privacy price per foot does not describe this work. Ground along low draws stays soft after rain, so brace posts are placed where the hole will hold. The house yard, if you want one, is wood, vinyl, or chain link and is quoted on its own. Telford is not a subdivision vinyl market, and it is not inside Johnson City. County questions go to Washington County, not to the city building desk.

Pasture first

Corners, braces, and farm gates matter more than picket style.

House yard second

A short privacy or dog run by the house is a separate, smaller fence.

Soft ground

Low draws stay wet. We do not set a corner brace in mud.

Do you build horse and cattle fence in Telford?

Yes. Horses get a fence they can see, such as rail. Cattle can use woven wire. We do not put horses on barbed wire.

Can you fence just the yard and leave the field for later?

Yes. We can look at both on one visit and build the yard first. The field stays its own number.

Does a Telford farm need a Johnson City fence permit?

No. Telford is Washington County, not the city. Confirm any county question with Washington County zoning. The city’s 7-foot residential note is not the default here.
