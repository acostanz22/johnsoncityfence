What fence work looks like here

We do not treat a Jonesborough street as if it were under the Johnson City zoning ordinance. The town has its own review, and some in-town properties sit in a local historic district where a fence on the frontage can need design approval before it goes up. Ask the town if your parcel is in that district. A 6-foot privacy fence that fits a rear yard in Johnson City may be the wrong height and the wrong material on a Jonesborough frontage. Farther out, toward Washington County acreage, the job turns into rail or wire. Those are quoted as a separate line from the house yard.

Narrow side yards

The gate width has to be exact. There is not much room to adjust after the concrete is in.

Town versus county

In-town historic frontages and outlying pasture are different rules and different fences.

Older trees

Roots along an established lot change post spacing. We lay the line around them instead of cutting through.

Do Johnson City’s fence heights apply in Jonesborough?

No. Jonesborough is its own town. Bring the local rule, or we will confirm it before we order a height. A city of Johnson City number copied onto a Jonesborough frontage is how a fence gets rejected.

Can you fence a small side yard in town?

Yes. Short runs are normal here. We measure the opening for the gate first, because a mower gate that is an inch too wide does not fit.

Do you also fence acreage outside Jonesborough?

Yes. House-yard wood or vinyl and a field fence on the rest of the parcel are two quotes. Tell us which one you want built first.
