Gate installation

Fence gate installation in Johnson City, TN

Walk gates and drive gates hung on posts that can carry the leaf. New openings, and repairs when a gate drags, sags, or will not latch.

When this fence is the right job

A gate is not a leftover panel. A 3-foot walk gate, a 4-foot mower gate, and a double drive gate put different loads on the posts. Most callbacks we see are a leaf hung on posts that were never sized for it, or hinges that have pulled out of rotten wood. If the posts are sound, we can rehang hardware. If they are not, the repair is new posts, not a bigger hinge.

How the work goes

  1. 1Measure the opening. The machine or the vehicle that has to pass, plus clearance for the swing or the slide.
  2. 2Check the posts. Sound posts can take new hardware. Rotted or loose posts get replaced before the leaf goes on.
  3. 3Choose the leaf. Wood, vinyl, aluminum, or steel to match the fence, in a width the opening can actually take.
  4. 4Hang and latch. Hinges, a latch you can use, and a ground clearance that survives the high spot.

Masonry posts, like the driveway in the photo, need hinges rated for that mount. We do not bolt a heavy steel leaf to a post that was built for a light aluminum section. If the whole fence is failing, start with fence repair so the gate is not the only part left standing.

What changes the estimate

We price the line on your lot. These are the pieces that move the number.

Opening width

A wider leaf needs heavier posts and hardware. Width is the first number we need.

Swing or slide

A swing gate needs room to open. A slide is for a driveway that cannot give up that arc.

Material

The leaf should match the fence. Mixing a steel gate onto a light vinyl line without the right posts fails.

Existing posts

Reusing posts saves money only when they are still solid. We will not pretend they are.

Questions about gate installation

How much does a fence gate cost in Johnson City?

Width, material, and whether the posts have to be replaced decide it. A walk gate on sound posts is a small job. A double drive gate with new posts is not. Tell us the opening and send a photo if you have one.

Can you fix a gate that drags on the ground?

Often. Sometimes the hinges can be reset. Sometimes the post has leaned in the clay and has to be reset or replaced. We look at the post before we sell you a new leaf you do not need.

Can you add a gate to a fence that does not have one?

Yes, if we can cut an opening and set posts that carry the new leaf. On a tensioned chain-link line, that also means rebuilding the terminal posts so the fabric stays tight.

Do you install double driveway gates?

Yes. They need posts sized for both leaves and hardware that holds them, such as drop rods. A single wide leaf is sometimes the better swing. We say which one fits the driveway.

Can a pool gate be added later?

A pool gate has to self-close and self-latch at the height the barrier code requires. We can add one when the fence itself meets the barrier rule. A short decorative gate on a 4-foot picket is not a pool gate.

Talk through the line

Call or send the town, the approximate length, and the gate count. We will tell you if the job is a repair, a replacement, or a different material than you started with.
