Commercial fencing

Commercial fence installation in Johnson City, TN

Heavier chain link, ornamental rail, and vehicle gates for yards, shops, and job sites. Specified for the use of the lot, not borrowed from a residential panel.

(423) 468-2697 Get a free quote

When this fence is the right job

Choose a commercial spec when trucks, equipment, or the public meet the fence. A storage yard along the Piney Flats and 11E corridor is a different fence from a backyard in Gray. Fabric gauge, post size, and the vehicle gate are the parts that change. A house-grade chain-link panel will not stand in for that. Ornamental rail is an option when the lot also has to look finished from the road.

How the work goes

  1. 1Define the use. Storage, equipment, a customer edge, or a temporary job site. The use sets the spec.
  2. 2Place the openings. Where trucks enter decides the gate before the long runs do.
  3. 3Set a commercial post layout. Heavier posts, concrete, and bracing at the corners and at the gate.
  4. 4Hang the gate and finish the line. Drop rods, cane bolts, or rollers, depending on the leaf. Then the fabric or rail.

We do not invent access-control systems or claim a fence is a security program. We build the barrier and the gate you can actually operate. If the site is in another town’s jurisdiction, the height rule may not be the Johnson City residential 7-foot note. We confirm the parcel.

What changes the estimate

We price the line on your lot. These are the pieces that move the number.

Spec

Commercial gauge and post size cost more than a residential chain-link run. That difference is the point.

Vehicle gates

A sliding or double-swing drive gate is often the largest line on the quote.

Length and paving

Coring pavement or working around a curb adds labor a grass yard does not have.

Screen

Privacy slats or a windscreen are optional and change the wind load on the posts.

Questions about commercial fencing

How much does a commercial fence cost in Johnson City?

Gate size and spec move it more than the long run does. A vehicle opening, heavier posts, and any coring through pavement are the usual cost drivers. We look at the lot before we price it.

Can you fence a lot in Piney Flats?

Yes. That corridor is commercial and industrial ground in Sullivan County, close enough to be in this service area. The fence is specified for trucks and yard use, not for a subdivision rear line.

Do you install barbed wire on commercial fences?

When the site and the rule allow it, a top strand can be part of a commercial chain-link spec. It is not something we add to a residential yard. We confirm the parcel before we include it.

Is a commercial fence just a taller version of a house fence?

No. Post size, fabric gauge, bracing, and the gate hardware are different. Using residential materials on a work yard is how a gate fails.

Can you match a black rail fence along a street frontage?

Yes. Ornamental rail is an option where the edge of the property faces customers. The secure yard behind it can still be chain link. They are two specs on one property.

Talk through the line

Call or send the town, the approximate length, and the gate count. We will tell you if the job is a repair, a replacement, or a different material than you started with.
