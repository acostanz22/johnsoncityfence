Chain-link fencing

Chain-link fence installation in Johnson City, TN

Galvanized and vinyl-coated chain link for yards, dogs, and light commercial lots. A practical fence when you need containment more than a solid screen.

When this fence is the right job

Choose chain link for a dog run, a side yard, a playground edge, or a lot where you still want to see through the fence. Add slats or a screen only where a visual break matters. It is not a horse fence, and a residential fabric gauge is not the spec for a storage yard that takes vehicle traffic. That heavier build is on the commercial fencing page.

How the work goes

  1. 1Set the use. Dog, yard line, or work lot. The use picks the height, the gauge, and whether the fabric is coated.
  2. 2Mark utilities. Tennessee 811 before the terminal posts go in. Corner posts and gate posts carry the tension.
  3. 3Set posts and pour. Terminal posts in concrete, line posts on spacing that holds the fabric tight in clay soil.
  4. 4Stretch, tie, and hang the gate. Fabric pulled tight, ties on the mesh, and a gate that does not drag.

Green and black vinyl-coated fabric is what we use when the fence sits in view of the house. Bare galvanized is fine on a back run or a work lot where appearance is not the point. A privacy slat changes the wind load, so we do not add a full screen without checking the post spacing.

What changes the estimate

We price the line on your lot. These are the pieces that move the number.

Height and gauge

A 4-foot yard fence and a 6-foot dog fence use different fabric and often different posts.

Coating

Vinyl-coated fabric costs more than galvanized and holds up better where you look at it every day.

Gates

A walk gate is simple. A double drive gate needs heavier posts and a drop rod or a cane bolt.

Screen or slats

Adding a visual screen is material and labor, and it changes how the fence takes wind.

Questions about chain-link fencing

How much does a chain-link fence cost in Johnson City?

Footage, height, coating, and gates set the price. A coated residential run costs more than bare galvanized. A commercial gauge with a vehicle gate is a different quote entirely. We measure before we number it.

Can you install chain link for a dog in a Johnson City yard?

Yes. Height and a latch the dog cannot open matter more than the color. Tell us the breed and whether the dog climbs or digs. A digger may need the fabric extended or buried at the bottom, which is part of the layout.

Do you offer vinyl-coated chain link?

Yes. Green and black are the coatings we install most on house lots. Galvanized is available when the fence is out of sight or the budget is the constraint.

Is chain link allowed in Johnson City?

The city does not publish a banned-materials list for ordinary residential fences. An HOA often does. If your subdivision restricts chain link, the covenants control. Send that sheet with the request.

Can you add privacy to an existing chain-link fence?

Often, if the posts and the top rail are still sound. Slats or a screen add wind load. If the line posts are already loose in the clay, we fix the posts before we add anything that catches air.

Talk through the line

Call or send the town, the approximate length, and the gate count. We will tell you if the job is a repair, a replacement, or a different material than you started with.
