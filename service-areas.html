Fence service area around Johnson City

We install and repair fences within about 25 miles of Johnson City. The places below are not copies of each other. Lot size, slope, farm lines, and which office governs the parcel all change the fence.

(423) 468-2697

Jonesborough

Jonesborough lots in town are often narrower than a new subdivision in Gray, with short side yards and older trees on the line. Outside town, the same drive reaches agricultural ground that wants a different fence.

Fence notes for Jonesborough

Elizabethton

Elizabethton sits in Carter County, with river-bottom ground that stays wet and hillside lots that climb toward rock. The post plan follows whichever of those the yard actually is.

Fence notes for Elizabethton

Gray

Gray is subdivision ground along US-11E in Washington County. The fence question is usually an HOA color, a rear privacy line, and posts that will hold in valley clay.

Fence notes for Gray

Boones Creek

Boones Creek mixes established yards northwest of Johnson City with lots that drop toward Boone Lake. Slope and the shoreline setback matter more here than they do on a flat street in town.

Fence notes for Boones Creek

Piney Flats

Piney Flats is commercial and industrial ground in Sullivan County, where I-26 meets US-11E. The fences here are yard perimeters and vehicle gates, not backyard privacy panels.

Fence notes for Piney Flats

Telford

Telford is rural Washington County. Fence work here is usually a long pasture line, a farm gate, and sometimes a shorter screen around the house.

Fence notes for Telford

Limestone

Limestone is agricultural Washington County. The fence jobs are property-line wire, paddocks, and lines that have to deal with bottomland that floods.

Fence notes for Limestone

Unicoi

Unicoi lots sit in the foothills, south of Johnson City. Digging hits rock, yards slope, and a fence has to be stepped instead of pulled flat down the hill.

Fence notes for Unicoi

Washington County

Washington County is the service area around Johnson City, and the fence rule changes when you leave the city limits. The ground is the part that stays familiar: valley clay, and rock as you climb.

Fence notes for Washington County
