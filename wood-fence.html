Wood fence installation in Johnson City, TN

Privacy, shadowbox, and board-on-board wood for yards that want a solid screen and a stain that matches the house. We measure the line, set posts for clay, and price each gate on its own.

When this fence is the right job

Choose wood when the job is a backyard screen, a stained match to the siding, or a repair on a wood line whose posts are still solid. A tall privacy run belongs on the side and rear. On a corner lot in the city, the 25-foot sight triangle stays at 3 feet, so a 6-foot wood wall does not belong across that view. Shaded older yards stay damp at the ground line, which is where untreated boards fail first.

How the work goes

  1. 1Walk the line. We note the slope, trees, gates, and who owns the property pins.
  2. 2Check the rule. City height, a historic overlay, and any HOA color sheet come before the lumber order.
  3. 3Set the posts. Posts go in concrete in the clay, crowned at the top so water does not sit against the wood.
  4. 4Hang the fence and the gate. Rails, boards, and a gate that latches without dragging on the ground.

Call Tennessee 811 before anyone digs. In a mature Johnson City yard the roots and the irrigation line are as likely to change the layout as the slope is. If an old fence is coming out, that labor is its own line on the estimate.

What changes the estimate

We price the line on your lot. These are the pieces that move the number.

Length and height

A 4-foot side return and a 6-foot rear screen are different amounts of lumber, even on the same lot.

Grade and trees

Stepping a panel down a slope, or working around a trunk, takes more posts than a flat run.

Gates

A 4-foot walk gate and a 10-foot drive opening are priced separately. The posts have to carry the swing.

Removal and finish

Pulling an old line, hauling it, and staining the new wood all move the number.

Questions about wood fencing

How much does a wood fence cost in Johnson City?

It depends on the footage, the height, the number of gates, and whether the old fence comes out. Clay, a slope, and a long carry from the driveway add labor. We measure the line and give you a number for that property. We do not quote a citywide price per foot.

Can you match the stain on a wood fence that is already there?

We can come close on a new section if you tell us the color, or we can stain the new run and leave the old boards as they are. An exact match on weathered wood is unlikely. If the posts under the old section have rotted, matching the color will not keep that part standing.

Do I need a permit for a 6-foot wood privacy fence in the city?

Published city standards allow a standard residential fence up to 7 feet without a structural permit. Over 7 feet needs a building permit. Corner sight triangles stay at 3 feet. A historic overlay or an HOA can add its own review. Confirm the parcel before the posts go in.

Will a wood fence rot in a shaded Johnson City yard?

Shade and summer humidity keep the ground line damp, and that is where rot starts. Concrete crowned away from the post, and a finish you actually maintain, slow that down. If you do not want that upkeep, a vinyl privacy fence is the closer fit.

Can you repair a few wood sections instead of replacing the line?

Yes, when the posts are still solid. We replace the failed boards or the gate and leave the sound run. If the posts have rotted or heaved, new boards on those posts will lean again, and that stretch should be rebuilt.
