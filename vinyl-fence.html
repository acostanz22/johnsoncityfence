Vinyl fence installation in Johnson City, TN

Privacy, picket, and ranch-rail vinyl for a straight line that does not need a yearly stain. A practical fit for humid summers and freeze-thaw winters around Johnson City.

When this fence is the right job

Choose vinyl when the HOA names a color, when you do not want to stain, or when a wood line in a damp yard has already rotted at the ground. Vinyl is a poor choice for a horse that leans on a fence, and it is not the heavy spec for a commercial vehicle gate. On a slope, panels are racked or stepped. We say which one the grade actually needs.

How the work goes

  1. 1Confirm the style. Privacy, picket, or rail, plus the color the HOA or the house actually calls for.
  2. 2Lay out the grade. A Johnson City side yard often drops toward the rear. We decide racked or stepped before the panels are ordered.
  3. 3Set the posts. Vinyl posts still go in concrete. Clay that holds water will move a shallow post the same way it moves a wood one.
  4. 4Assemble and gate. Panels, caps, and a gate leaf that clears the high spot in the yard.

Vinyl does not rot, and it also does not hide a bad layout. A panel forced onto a steep grade looks wavy. We would rather step that section than stretch it. Rails for a pasture are on the farm fencing page, because a house-yard vinyl panel is the wrong product for livestock.

What changes the estimate

We price the line on your lot. These are the pieces that move the number.

Style and height

A 6-foot privacy panel costs more material than a 4-foot picket on the same footage.

Color

White, tan, and gray are common. A special color can change the lead time and the price.

Slope

Stepping a grade uses more posts and more cuts than a level run along a flat rear line.

Gates and removal

Each opening is its own leaf and hardware. Hauling an old wood fence is extra labor.

Questions about vinyl fencing

How much does a vinyl fence cost in Johnson City?

Privacy vinyl usually costs more per foot than a basic wood line and less upkeep after that. Height, color, slope, and gates move the quote. We price the measured line, not a catalog rate.

Can vinyl handle a freeze and a hot summer here?

Yes. The panels are made for outdoor temperature swings. The part that fails in this climate is usually a post set too shallow in wet clay, not the vinyl itself. Posts go in concrete, crowned at the top.

Do you offer vinyl if the HOA only allows a certain color?

Yes, when that color is a real product we can order. Send the HOA sheet with the request so the quote uses the right panel. We will not guess from the fence next door.

Is vinyl a good farm fence?

Ranch-rail vinyl can mark a line or a light paddock. It is not what we use for cattle pressure or a horse that cribs and leans. Those jobs are rail or wire built for the animal.

Can you put vinyl on a sloped Johnson City lot?

Yes. Mild slopes can be racked so the bottom follows the grade. Steeper drops are stepped. Forcing a panel to do both is how you get a gap a dog walks under.
