Aluminum fencing

Aluminum fence installation in Johnson City, TN

Open aluminum picket for front yards, pool barriers, and lines that have to follow a slope. It stays straight in humidity and does not ask for a paint cycle the way steel does.

When this fence is the right job

Choose aluminum when you want a boundary people can see through: a front yard, a pool enclosure, or a side line that should not feel like a wall. It is the wrong product for a privacy screen and the wrong product for a heavy drive gate that needs a steel frame. Ornamental steel is on the metal fencing page. Aluminum racks well on the grade changes common on Johnson City side yards.

How the work goes

  1. 1Pick the height and the picket spacing. Front yards and corner sight triangles stay lower. Pool barriers follow the adopted pool code, not a decorative height.
  2. 2Check the grade. Aluminum sections can rack. We still mark where a step is cleaner than a rack.
  3. 3Set posts in concrete. Even a light fence moves if the clay lets the post go. Concrete and a crowned top are standard.
  4. 4Hang sections and gates. A pool gate needs self-closing hardware. A yard gate needs a latch you can use with one hand.

Black is the finish we install most. It reads as a line, not a wall, which is why it shows up on frontages where a privacy panel would block the house. If the job is a solid screen, look at privacy fencing instead.

What changes the estimate

We price the line on your lot. These are the pieces that move the number.

Height and style

A 4-foot open picket and a 5-foot pool barrier are different sections and different hardware.

Grade

Racking is included in a normal slope. A steep drop that has to be stepped takes more posts.

Gates

A self-closing pool gate is a different piece of hardware than a simple walk gate.

Footage

Open aluminum is priced on the measured run, including corners and the posts at each end.

Questions about aluminum fencing

How much does an aluminum fence cost in Johnson City?

It is usually more per foot than chain link and less maintenance than painted steel. Height, gates, and slope decide the number. Pool hardware adds cost because the gate has to close itself.

Can aluminum be used as a pool fence?

Yes, when the height, the picket spacing, and the self-closing gate meet the pool barrier code the city has adopted. A decorative 3-foot picket is not automatically a legal pool barrier. We spec the barrier to the rule, then build that.

Will aluminum rust in East Tennessee humidity?

Aluminum does not rust the way bare steel does. It can stain if a sprinkler throws minerals on it, and a scratched finish should be touched up. It does not need the repaint cycle of ornamental steel.

Do I need a permit for an aluminum front-yard fence?

A standard residential fence up to 7 feet does not need a structural permit inside the city. A corner sight triangle still cannot exceed 3 feet. Front-yard height can also be limited by an HOA even when the city allows more. Confirm both.

Can you install aluminum on a hill?

Yes. That is one of the reasons people pick it here. Sections rack to follow a mild grade. Very steep ground is stepped so the bottom does not open a gap.

Talk through the line

Call or send the town, the approximate length, and the gate count. We will tell you if the job is a repair, a replacement, or a different material than you started with.
